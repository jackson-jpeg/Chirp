ChirpChirps 2.0

Everything it does, with no signal

Bold: drawn on this canvas. The rest ship in 2.0 too, unless marked later.

Talk

  • Hold to talk, or the Action button, or squeeze AirPods
  • Lock screen and Dynamic Island talk button
  • Whisper: hold a friend's bird to talk to just them
  • Hands-free mode for driving and biking
  • Your own chirp plays when you start talking, so people know it's you without looking

Hear

  • Live captions on every message, made on the phone
  • Catch up: everything you missed out of range, played in order at 1.5x
  • Captions-only mode for loud concerts or a sleeping tent
  • Translate a friend's words into your language (later)

Find

  • Nearby: who hears you directly or through a friend
  • Find a friend with a pointing arrow, down to a few meters
  • Ping: makes a lost friend's phone chirp out loud
  • Last heard: where and when a friend dropped out of range

Plan

  • Meet-up spot with a time, who's coming and how far each is
  • Chat with voice and text, "Heard by" ticks
  • Small photos over the line ("this is the bar")
  • Battery level of each friend, so you know who to worry about

Stay safe

  • Roll call: one tap asks everyone "you OK?"
  • I need help: a loud alert that every phone passes along
  • Block anyone, location off unless you say yes each time

You and your flock

  • Make your bird: colors, tuft and chirp
  • Join by holding phones together, or a three-word code
  • Apple Watch: talk and find from your wrist
  • No account and no phone number

Two things the build has to prove first

The app keeps hearing your friends with the phone locked and no signal. The lock screen talk button depends on it. And Find's pointing arrow needs iPhones with the precision-finding chip (iPhone 11 and later), with a plain distance as the fallback.
