CH 01 · Peer-to-peer mesh

No signal? Still works.

ChirpChirps turns the iPhones around you into a private walkie-talkie mesh. Your voice hops phone to phone over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi — no cell towers, no internet, no accounts, no servers to trust.

CH 02 · How it works

Three steps.
Zero infrastructure.

01

Open the app

No sign-up, no account, no phone number. Pick a callsign and you're on the mesh.

02

Press and hold

The PTT button transmits your voice to everyone on your channel. Release to listen.

03

Messages relay

Out of range? Your signal hops through the phones between you. Five devices cover about a kilometer.

CH 04 · Capabilities

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

01

Push-to-Talk

Opus voice that adapts to the link, up to 24 kbps. Hold the button and talk.

02

Mesh Relay

Every phone is a relay node. Packets hop device to device with TTL routing and automatic deduplication.

03

Text Chat

Type when you can't talk. Messages ride the same mesh as voice for a fraction of the bandwidth.

04

Live Transcription

On-device speech recognition captions incoming voice as it plays. Read what you couldn't hear.

05

Encrypted Channels

AES-256-GCM between channel members, Ed25519 device identity, keys held in the iOS Keychain.

06

Voice Messages

Friend out of range? The message waits on your phone and delivers itself the moment they're reachable.

07

Photo Sharing

Send pictures straight to the phones around you. Compressed for the mesh, uploaded nowhere.

08

Offline Maps

Download map regions ahead of time, then watch your group move across them without a signal.

09

Block & Report

Silence any peer instantly. Report abuse from inside the app — no server needed to protect yourself.

CH 06 · Built for

Where the
network isn't.

/ 01

Backcountry

No towers for miles. Your group stays connected across ridgelines and valleys.

/ 02

Crowded events

Eighty thousand people overwhelm a cell tower. A mesh gets stronger with every device that joins it.

/ 03

Travel abroad

No SIM, no roaming, no plan. Talk to the people you came with, in any country.

/ 04

Worksites & campuses

Warehouses, film sets, ski patrol, festivals — teams that work close together and can't count on coverage.

ChirpChirps is not a substitute for emergency services and should not be relied on for life-safety communication. Range and delivery depend on radio conditions, device placement, and how many devices are nearby.
