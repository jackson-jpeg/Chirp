What ChirpChirps 2.0 is built with.

Nunito

The typeface in the app and on this site, by Vernon Adams, Cyreal and Jacques Le Bailly.

License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (full text)

github.com/googlefonts/nunito

Opus

The voice codec: what you say is packed into small 20 millisecond frames before it goes to the phones nearby. By the Xiph.Org Foundation and contributors, packaged for Swift by Alta Software.

License: BSD 3-Clause

opus-codec.org · github.com/alta/swift-opus

Apple frameworks

  • Network: talking to the phones nearby over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi
  • CoreBluetooth: texts, roll calls and help reaching a locked phone
  • NearbyInteraction: the arrow in Find, on iPhones with Ultra Wideband
  • Speech: captions, made on your phone
  • CryptoKit: locked birdhouses, whispers and signatures
  • AVFoundation: recording and playing voice
  • Core Location: where you are, only during a check-in you start

This page grows as the app does: anything else 2.0 ships with is listed here before it is out.
