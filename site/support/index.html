How does it work with no signal?

The phones talk to each other directly, over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. No cell tower, Wi-Fi network or internet is involved. When a friend is too far for your phone, a phone in between can pass your voice along.

How far does it reach?

It depends on the place: open slopes carry further than a crowded festival field or the steel decks of a ship. The Nearby screen shows who can hear you right now, and whether directly or through someone's phone.

Who can hear me?

Everyone in your birdhouse. A birdhouse is locked with its own key, and only phones that joined with its invite link can hear it or read its texts.

Does it work with my phone locked?

Partly. Texts, roll calls, help and pings reach a locked phone over Bluetooth and show as notifications. To talk and listen, open the app: voice only works while ChirpChirps is on screen, so it never listens in your pocket.

Why does it ask for Bluetooth, Local Network and notifications?

Local Network is how the phones nearby find each other, Bluetooth is how messages reach a locked phone, and notifications are how you see them. All three are asked the first time you start talking, never when the app opens.

Does it need my location?

No. Location is off unless you check in, and you are asked each time. A check-in shows your friends where you are for 15 minutes, then stops.

How do I block someone?

On the Nearby screen, tap the person and choose Block. They disappear from your screens and nothing of theirs reaches you.

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned back on in Control Center. Cellular is never used.
