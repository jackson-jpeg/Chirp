Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026. This version describes ChirpChirps 2.0.

Overview

ChirpChirps lets you talk and text with friends nearby, phone to phone. The app has no accounts and uses no servers, so it collects nothing and has nothing to send us. Your voice and messages go directly between phones over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, never through the internet.

Data we collect

The app collects none. The ChirpChirps app does not collect, store on our side, transmit to us, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics and no telemetry.

This website is separate from the app. The website counts visits, as described under Visitor analytics, and keeps the email addresses of people who ask to hear when the app is out, as described under Release list.

What phones nearby see

Because there is no server, the phones around you are how ChirpChirps works. Here is what they get.

  • Your name, your bird and your public keys are sent every few seconds to every phone in range running ChirpChirps, so they can show you on their Nearby screen and check that messages from you are really yours. Your private keys never leave your phone.
  • The Nearby line is open. Talk and texts on it can be heard and read by anyone in range running ChirpChirps, like a walkie-talkie.
  • Locked flocks and whispers are sealed. A locked flock is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and only phones that joined with its invite link can hear or read it. A whisper is encrypted for its one recipient. Phones that pass a sealed message along cannot open it.
  • An invite link holds your flock's key. Anyone who has the link can hear the flock, so send it only to the people you mean to invite. The key sits after the "#" in the link, which browsers never send to our server, and the page it opens has no analytics, so the key goes only where you send it.
  • Friends' phones hold messages for you. If you are out of range, phones in your flock keep the texts, roll calls, meet-ups and help calls you missed (still sealed, for at most 24 hours) and hand them over when you are back.
  • Battery level and Find go to friends only: people in a locked flock with you, never strangers nearby.

Permissions

Each is asked the first time you use what needs it, never when the app opens, and everything is processed on your phone:

  • Microphone: only while you hold to talk
  • Local Network: to reach the phones nearby
  • Bluetooth: so texts, roll calls, help and pings reach you with your phone locked
  • Notifications: to show those while the app is closed
  • Speech Recognition: captions, made on your phone; your voice is never sent anywhere for them
  • Nearby Interaction: the arrow in Find, only while Find is open
  • Location: optional, only during a check-in you start (see Location sharing)

Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Location sharing

Location is the one permission that can put something about you in front of other people, so it works differently from the rest.

  • It is off until you ask for it. ChirpChirps never requests location access at launch. The request appears only after you open check-in and read what sharing involves, and you can decline it.
  • You check in by hand, every time. Your position is shared only after you tap Check In. There is no automatic check-in, no background sharing, and no setting that shares your position all the time.
  • Each check-in lasts 15 minutes. Sharing then stops on its own. It also stops the moment you tap Stop, if you leave the app, or if you turn location access off. It never restarts by itself.
  • Only your flock sees it. Your position goes only to a locked flock you are in, sealed with that flock's key. Other phones may pass it along on the way but cannot read it, and it never goes to everyone nearby. It is not uploaded, not stored by us, and not shared with anyone else: we run no servers that could receive it.
  • Declining costs you nothing else. Talking, flocks, texts and everything else work without location.
  • You can block anyone. A blocked person is dropped entirely on your phone (voice, messages and position alike) and disappears from your screens.

Release list

If you enter your email address under "Coming to iPhone", we keep that address and the time you entered it, and nothing else: not your IP address, not your browser. It is stored on our own server and backed up there and on a computer of ours, and it is shared with no one.

We use it for one thing: one email on the day ChirpChirps is out on the App Store. After that email has gone out, we delete the list and its backups, your address with them. To be taken off the list before then, email support@unpossible.studio.

Visitor analytics

We count visits to this website (chirpchirps.com) with Umami, running on our own server, so nothing goes to an analytics company. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. For each visit it records the page, the referring site, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and an approximate location (country, region and city, worked out from your IP address; the address itself is not stored). Visit records are deleted after 24 months. This applies to the website only; the app collects nothing.

Server logs

Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser, page requested) for 13 months, for security and to count visits.

Cookies and Do Not Track

This website sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser's storage, and loads nothing from other companies' servers (its fonts are served from chirpchirps.com). Because our visit counting does not follow you to other sites and sets no cookies, the site treats every visitor the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Third-party services

ChirpChirps includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking, and the app makes no internet connections at all.

Data storage

Your messages, your bird and your settings are kept on your phone with iOS data protection, and your keys in the iOS Keychain. Deleting the app deletes them.

Age rating and children's privacy

ChirpChirps is rated 18+ and is not directed to children. Because the app can show a user's location to other people nearby, it is intended for adults only. The app has no accounts and no servers, and collects no personal information from any user. The website's release list is not meant for children; if a child's address reaches it, email us and we delete it.

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date.
