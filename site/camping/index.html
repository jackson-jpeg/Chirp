How it helps

The moments it is for.

Back at camp by dark?

Roll call asks everyone if they are OK with one tap. "I need help" is one tap too.

Grabbing firewood

Hold to talk and the group hears you. Let go to listen. One person talks at a time, so nobody talks over anyone.

Where is the tent?

Ping a friend and their phone chirps, so you can walk to the sound. Find shows how far away they are.

Phone in your pocket

With your phone locked, texts and pings still come in over Bluetooth and show as notifications.

Good to know

Before you go.

  • Everyone needs ChirpChirps on their own iPhone. Start a birdhouse and send the invite link at home.
  • Trees, hills and rock shorten the range. Nearby shows who can hear you right now.
  • ChirpChirps is not a replacement for emergency services. Tell someone at home where you are going.

ChirpChirps is not a substitute for emergency services and should not be relied on for life-safety communication. How far it reaches depends on the place, where the phones are, and how many are nearby.
