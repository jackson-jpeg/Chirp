How it works

Three steps, then just talk.

1

Make your bird

Pick a name, your colors and the chirp your friends hear when you start talking.

2

Start a flock

Name it and send friends the invite link. Only people with the link can hear it.

3

Hold to talk

Let go to send. Everyone in the flock hears you, and the phones in between pass it along.

What it does

Made for groups out of signal.

Whisper

Talk to one friend instead of the whole flock. Only they can hear it.

Captions

Every voice message can be read. Captions are made on your phone, and there is a captions only mode for when you cannot play sound.

Catch up

Out of range for a while? Your friends' phones hold what you missed and play it back in order when you return.

Ping

Make a friend's phone chirp loudly, so a lost phone or a lost friend can be found.

Roll call

Ask "everyone OK?" with one tap. "I need help" goes out ahead of everything else.

Meet-up spots

Pick a place and a time, and see who is coming, who is running late and who cannot make it.

Block

Block anyone. They vanish from your screens and nothing of theirs reaches you.

Location is yours

Off unless you check in, and asked every time. A check-in lasts 15 minutes.

With the phone locked

Texts, roll calls, help and pings still reach you, over Bluetooth, as notifications.

To talk and listen

Open the app. Voice works while ChirpChirps is on screen, so it is never listening in your pocket.
