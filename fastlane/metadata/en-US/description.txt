ChirpChirps is a walkie-talkie for your group when there is no signal. Phones talk straight to each other, so it works on a ski lift, at a festival, on a cruise ship or at a campsite with no bars.

Start a flock, give it a name, and send the invite link to your friends. Then hold the big button and talk. Everyone in your flock who is close by hears you.

TALK
• Hold to talk, let go to listen. One person talks at a time, so nobody talks over anyone.
• Live captions of what your friends say, made on your phone. When it is too loud to listen, switch to captions only.
• Your own bird, in your colors, with its own chirp when you start talking.

TEXT
• Texts to your flock, with ticks when they arrive.
• With your phone locked, texts from your flock still come in over Bluetooth and show as notifications.

FIND YOUR FRIENDS
• Nearby shows who can hear you right now.
• Find shows how far away a friend is, and which way to walk on iPhones that support it.
• Share where you are with your flock for 15 minutes. It is off until you ask, and it asks every time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no phone number, no servers. Nothing you say goes to the internet.
• Your flock is locked with its own key. Only people you invite can hear it.
• Block anyone nearby and they disappear from your app.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Everyone needs ChirpChirps on their own iPhone, close by. Range depends on walls, crowds and terrain.
• Talking works with the app open. With the phone locked, texts and alerts still arrive, and the Lock Screen card opens the app so you can reply.
• ChirpChirps is not a replacement for emergency services.
