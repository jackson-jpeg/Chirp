ChirpChirps turns the iPhones around you into a private walkie-talkie mesh — no cell towers, no Wi-Fi network, no internet, no accounts.

Hold to talk and your voice reaches everyone on your channel over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. Out of range of someone? Other ChirpChirps devices relay your message hop by hop across the mesh.

TALK
• Push-to-talk voice with live on-device transcription of incoming audio
• Channels keep groups separate — join General or create your own
• AES-256-GCM encryption between channel members

MESSAGES
• Text chat that works with zero infrastructure
• Voice messages with store-and-forward delivery — nearby devices carry a message until its recipient is reachable
• Share photos directly, device to device

MAP
• See where the peers on your channel are — position is shared only with nearby peers, never uploaded
• There is no server: everything you say and send stays on the devices involved

WHO IT'S FOR
Hiking groups past the last bar of signal, festivals and stadiums with overloaded networks, ski trips, campuses, warehouses, disaster drills — anywhere people are near each other and the internet isn't.

ChirpChirps needs at least two devices with the app installed to communicate. Range depends on environment and grows with every device that joins the mesh. ChirpChirps is not a substitute for emergency services.
