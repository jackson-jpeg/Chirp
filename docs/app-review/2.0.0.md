## 1. Review notes (NOT MET)

Draft. Before marking this MET, check every line against the build being submitted.

**What it is.** ChirpChirps is push-to-talk and text for friends who are near each
other where there is no cell signal: a ski trip, a festival, a boat. Phones talk to each
other directly over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and pass messages along. There are
no accounts and no servers.

**Who it is for.** Groups of friends and families out together, out of signal.

**Login.** No account is needed. There is no sign-up and no phone number, so there are
no credentials to give.

**How to review it with one device: Demo Mode.** On the first screen (Welcome), under the
"Start a birdhouse" and "Join a birdhouse" buttons and the line "No account. No phone number.",
tap the text link **"Try it with pretend friends"**. The app opens on Talk in a demo birdhouse
with three pretend friends (Maya, Jon and Priya) who talk and text on a one-minute loop.
A banner at the top of Talk reads "Demo birdhouse. Nothing leaves this phone." Its
**"Leave demo"** button ends the demo and returns to Welcome. While the demo runs, the
radio is sandboxed: nothing is sent to any real phone. Demo messages go through the same
receive path as real ones, so Talk, Chat (with delivery ticks) and Nearby behave as they
would with real people. One phone is enough to review everything a birdhouse does: the demo
birdhouse is a real locked birdhouse, so ping, battery, Find, check-in and delivery ticks all work
in it. **Roll call:** on Talk, tap the birdhouse's name, then "Roll call: everyone OK?", then
"Ask everyone": Maya and Jon answer OK, Priya (out of range) does not. "I need help"
asks everyone in the birdhouse for help; tapping it again cancels. Jon also starts a roll call once a
minute in the demo, shown as a line under Talk's header. (Start a birdhouse and Join a birdhouse can also be tried with one phone, see below; with
nobody else nearby, Talk then shows only you.)

**How a birdhouse is started and joined.** On Welcome, "Start a birdhouse" asks for the birdhouse's
name, then Make your bird, then Talk. The birdhouse is locked with a fresh key. To invite
friends, tap the birdhouse's name at the top of Talk and choose **Invite friends**: the iOS
share sheet sends a link (https://chirpchirps.com/join/#...). The link carries the birdhouse's
key after the "#", which a browser never sends to a server, and the join page has no
analytics, so the key stays on the phones. A friend who opens the link in the app goes
straight to Make your bird, then Talk, in that birdhouse. If the link opens the website
instead, it has an "Open in ChirpChirps" button; failing that, "Join a birdhouse" on Welcome
(or in the birdhouse menu on Talk) has a field and a Paste button for the link. The birdhouse
menu also has **Leave**. Pretend friends appear only in Demo Mode.

**How to use it with two devices.** Install on two iPhones near each other. On the first,
tap "Start a birdhouse", name it, make a bird, then Talk > the birdhouse's name > Invite friends,
and send the link to the second phone (Messages or AirDrop). On the second, open the link
(or paste it on "Join a birdhouse"), make a bird. Then hold the big button on Talk to talk. Permissions (Microphone, Local Network, Bluetooth, Notifications) are asked on the
first tap that needs them, never at launch.

**External services.** None. The app makes no internet connections: no servers, no
analytics, no third-party SDKs that phone home. Captions use on-device speech recognition
only (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`).

**Regions.** It works the same in every region. Nothing in it depends on the country,
the carrier or an internet connection.

**Background.** Voice runs only while the app is open (guideline 2.5.4: no `audio`
background mode). With the phone locked, texts, roll calls, help calls, pings and
"someone is talking" alerts arrive over Bluetooth LE (`bluetooth-central` and
`bluetooth-peripheral`) and show as notifications. The Lock Screen card's Talk button
opens the app; nothing talks while locked.

**Blocking (5.1.2(i)).** Nearby lists everyone in range. Each person has Block, which
drops their packets both ways and removes them from the wire, Chat and Nearby.

**Location (5.1.1, 5.1.2(i)).** Location is only used for a check-in, and only While
Using the App (no "Always", no background location). Nearby has a "Share where you are"
card that reads "Off · asks every time". Tapping Share shows our own question first, every
time ("Share where you are with <birdhouse>?", with "Share for 15 minutes" and "Not now"); the
iOS location prompt appears only after "Share for 15 minutes", the first time. A check-in
lasts 15 minutes and stops sooner on Stop, on leaving the app, or when location access is
turned off. It never restarts by itself. The position goes only to a locked birdhouse the
person is in (sealed with the birdhouse's key), never to everyone nearby; with no locked birdhouse,
Share explains that and asks nothing. To see it: Demo Mode's birdhouse is a locked birdhouse, so
Nearby > Share works there. Declining costs nothing else. (Check this paragraph against
the submitted build before submitting.)

## 4. In-app purchases (NOT MET)

One non-consumable, **Big Birdhouse** (`com.jacksonmsanger.chirpchirp.bigflock`, $4.99 suggested;
Jackson, Inbox chirp-2-0-revenue-shape, "softer"). It adds: birdhouses you start grow past 4
birds, three more bird colors (Teal, Lilac, Orange) and one more chirp (Wren), and kept
birdhouses: joining another keeps the one you were in, to switch back from Talk's birdhouse
menu (free keeps one and asks before leaving; ChirpCore `SavedBirdhouses`). Everything
else is free, joiners never pay, and nothing stops working without it. Code:
`v2/App/Sources/App/Store.swift`, ChirpCore `BigBirdhouse`, sheet `Screens/BigBirdhouseView.swift`,
with Restore purchase in the sheet. Local test file `v2/App/StoreKit/Chirp.storekit`.
